## Cleanup policy and purpose review

Retire 20 completed version-specific handoff workflows and the old 8.9 branch-prune workflow. Replace the obsolete 7.1.7 cleanup implementation with one guarded maintenance workflow. Restrict push validation to main/production; PR updates retain their own full validation, avoiding duplicate branch-push cancellations. The separate scouting-evaluation workflow was already consolidated in 9.1.1. Preserve compatibility workers, manifests/icons, the original launch asset and V1 test fixture for installed-app migration and parity verification.

Only unchanged exact heads of merged main-targeted PRs may be deleted automatically. Protected branches, open PR branches and unreviewed/changed heads remain. `main`, `production` and `runtime-state` are always preserved. Cleanup requires a matching published release, production tag and successful Pages rollout.
